<h1 align="center">3ColRef — A Simple Reference Image Viewer</h1>

<p align="center">
  <b>Elevate Your Creative Workflow</b><br>
  <i>A minimal, high-performance reference viewer designed to stay out of your way.</i>
</p>

---

### **Core Features**

*   **Live Cloud Collaboration** — Share your workspace in real-time. Generate a secure cloud code to let peers instantly view your scene, see your cursor, and watch live crop/scale transformations—all powered by a dedicated, low-latency deployment.
*   **Premium Interaction** — Move, scale, rotate, crop, and flip your reference images with smooth, intuitive controls.
*   **Intelligent Arrangement** — Instantly scale images to matching dimensions or arrange them horizontally and vertically for optimal space usage.
*   **Minimalist Workspace** — Enable **Always-on-Top** mode and hide the title bar to let the 3ColRef window unobtrusively float above your digital canvas.
*   **Visual History** — A reimagined welcome screen featuring **vertical cards** and **live scene thumbnails** for effortless project management.
*   **Engineered for Speed** — Optimized startup and resource management with a lightweight, standalone executable (~62MB).

### **Installation**

1.  **Download** the `3ColRef.exe` from the Assets below.
2.  **Launch** — No installation required. Simply run the file and start creating.
