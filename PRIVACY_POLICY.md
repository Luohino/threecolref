7. About cookies
----------------
We use cookies to help improve our website visitors' experience and the use of our services. Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer by websites that you visit.

There are two types of cookies, we use both:
* **Session cookies**, which are temporarily stored on the computer while a guest visits the web page.
* **Persistent cookies**, which are stored in the computer until manually deleted.

You can turn off cookies in your browser's security settings. If you turn off Cookies you should be aware that the experience of our site may be worse.

Your explicit consent is required to use cookies. We ask for your consent at your first visit on our website.
